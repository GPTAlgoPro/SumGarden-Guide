Privacy

Practice data

Questions, answers, progress, review items and settings are processed on your device. Tally Turtle has no app account, cloud sync, advertising or third-party analytics. Your system settings control device backups.

Purchases

The App Store handles local pricing, payments and restoring purchases. Tally Turtle reads verified entitlements through StoreKit and does not receive payment card details. Unlocked lessons can be practiced offline.

Managing your data

Reset learning records in Settings or delete the app to remove local app data. Apple manages your purchase history. Resetting learning records preserves your purchase.

This website

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub handles site visits and support issues under its privacy statement.

Contact the developer

Email support at sunkai4u@gmail.com.

When you email support, the developer receives your email address and message to respond to your request. Posts on GitHub Issues are public.

Share your device model, app version and steps in a public issue. Keep personal and payment details private.
