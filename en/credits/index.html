Tally and the Luo Shu square

Tally draws on the traditional story of the Luo River turtle. Tally Turtle created the character, the kites, koi, swallows and lanterns in the garden, and the music, motion and haptic feedback used during practice. After a round, explore the Luo Shu square to see how each row, column and diagonal adds to 15.
